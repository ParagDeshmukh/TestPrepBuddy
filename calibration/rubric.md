# Blind review rubric

Synthetic draft material. Use this exercise to calibrate judgment for the Grade 4 practice project. The Content Lead owns the later suitability decision (D5).

1. Open `blind-review.html` locally. Read the stem, solve from the evidence and record every defensible choice. Describe the rule in your own words before opening the answer sheet.
2. Rate each dimension. **Pass** means the condition in the table is met. **Revise** means the item is solvable but needs a specific improvement. **Reject** means the defect prevents a reliable answer or fair scoring. For a text-only item, select **N/A** for diagram readability.
3. After recording the blind solve, compare it with the worked reasoning, the stored key and the structural result in the answer sheet. Record any discrepancy and the final disposition. A blind solve alone cannot detect a mistyped stored key.
4. Reject an item with a missing diagram, an unusable key, or two defensible answers. If every applicable dimension passes and the key agrees with the blind solve, pass the exercise. Otherwise record the required revision and repeat blind review with an independent reviewer after correction.

| Dimension | What earns a pass | Evidence to record when it fails |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rule | One stated or inferable operation explains each step and predicts the next. | Point to the first step the proposed rule cannot explain. |
| Uniqueness | Exactly one rendered option satisfies the rule and its letter matches the stored key at reconciliation. | List all matching letters, or the observed answer and conflicting key. |
| Distractors | Each of the other three choices reflects a different, explainable error. | Identify which choice also works, is arbitrary or duplicates another error. |
| Wording | The reader can identify the task, the reading order and what counts as an answer. | Quote the ambiguous phrase and propose a precise edit. |
| Diagram readability | Every required panel and option loads. Corners, marks and ordering stay distinguishable at narrow and wide widths without incidental visual clues. | Record viewport, panel and letter. Name the missing, clipped or indistinguishable feature. |

For an independent session, give the reviewer only the blind HTML, this rubric and the assets folder. Keep the manifest, fixtures, checker output and answer sheet with the facilitator until reconciliation.
